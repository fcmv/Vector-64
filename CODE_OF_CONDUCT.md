# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Vector-64's issues, reviews, discussions or other
project spaces is expected to treat others with respect. Participation is open
regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the code, evidence and user problem. Critique ideas without attacking people.
- Give newcomers enough context to reproduce a problem or improve a contribution.
- Respect differing experience, communication styles and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections, credit others' work and respect privacy.
- Keep disagreements focused and allow people to leave a conversation.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation,
doxxing and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Deliberately disrupting
reviews or retaliating against a good-faith report is also unacceptable. Technical
criticism, declining a proposal and asking for evidence are normal project work.

## Reporting and privacy

For public content, use GitHub's report controls where available. If a private
project contact is needed, open an issue titled **Private contact request** with
no incident details and ask the maintainer to arrange a private channel. Do not
publish personal information, private messages or sensitive evidence to obtain help.
The project does not yet publish a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address.

Use [GitHub's abuse reporting route](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for platform abuse or concerns involving the sole maintainer. GitHub's process is
separate from project moderation; this project cannot promise an independent
internal appeal while it has only one recorded maintainer. Vulnerability reports
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

The maintainer reviews available context and can ask participants to change
behavior, remove abusive content, pause or lock a discussion, or restrict project
participation. Responses should be proportionate to severity, repetition and
impact. Serious threats or exposure of private information may require immediate
restriction without a prior warning.

Explain the rule and reason for an action when doing so will not expose a reporter
or other private information. Share report details only with people handling the
case. Maintainers are subject to the same standards. A participant may request
reconsideration with new context through the agreed private channel; when another
uninvolved maintainer is available, that person should review the request.
